## 🎯 Goal

On a chokidar watcher error, broadcast a sticky SSE `degraded` event (replayed to later-connecting clients) and have `useProjectData` treat it as live-updates-off — `liveDisconnected` + poll fallback, no reconnect while the stream is open.
