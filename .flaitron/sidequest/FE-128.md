# FE-128 | viz-host-pin

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 📌 Sidequest (filed 2026-10-11)

## Idea

FE-127.3 added a loopback peer-address guard to `guarded`, but it covers `/api/*` only. Under `vite --host`, Vite's own serving on port 5120 (the page, `/src/*` modules, `/@fs/` files within `server.fs.allow`, the HMR websocket) stays reachable from the network, and `allowedHosts` does not stop a remote client that sets `Host: localhost`. Fix: pin `server.host` to loopback in `viz/vite.config.ts`, or add one loopback filter ahead of every `server.middlewares` route. Then drop SECURITY.md §Visualizer's "do not run with `--host`" caveat.
